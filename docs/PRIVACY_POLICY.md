### 1. Introduction
At **Oozox ("Zahaby")**, we take your privacy and data security very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information, what permissions are requested, and how we protect your data in full compliance with **Google Play Developer Policies**.

### 2. Data Storage & Offline-First Architecture
- **Oozox** is designed with an **Offline-First** architecture.
- All financial records, project budgets, expenses, gold and silver transactions, and invoices are stored locally on your device using a secured local database (`Room Database / Local Encrypted Storage`).
- **We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal or financial data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.**

### 3. Permissions Requested & Information Usage
The application may request the following device permissions solely to provide core functionalities:
1. **Camera & Photo Storage:** To capture and attach photos of project invoices, gold weighing scales, and receipts locally on your device.
2. **Biometrics (Fingerprint / Face Unlock):** To enable secure app lock on your device. Biometric verification is processed entirely by the Android OS locally and is never sent to any server.
3. **Notifications (Push Notifications / FCM):** To send essential system alerts such as metal price updates and project milestone reminders.
4. **Internet Access:** Required to fetch live metal rates and receive real-time push notifications.

### 4. Third-Party Services
The app utilizes reputable third-party SDKs that process data strictly in accordance with their privacy guidelines:
- **Google Play Services:** For app integrity, licensing, and stability.
